Build Rethinksoft, an AI-native startup foundry for technical founders with working B2B software. The public promise is: Build your startup. We fund the progress.

The core thesis is that software has become cheaper to build, so early startup capital can become smaller, faster, more experimental, and tied to verified learning. Rethinksoft should not present AI as an autonomous investor. AI is the diligence and monitoring infrastructure. Accountable humans approve capital.

Target one or two technical founders who have already shipped a working B2B product, completed at least five genuine customer conversations, earn less than $5,000 MRR, can reach paying customers within 30 days, and are comfortable providing verifiable evidence.

Design the public experience around this journey:

1. Apply in about 15 minutes with the founder, product, customer, evidence, current constraint, and requested experiment.
2. Specialized Founder, Market, Product, Technical, Distribution, and Traction agents analyze the company independently.
3. A Red Team agent argues against the investment and identifies the assumptions most likely to fail.
4. Rethink Score presents an explainable provisional score, evidence confidence, positive case, concerns, and next proof point.
5. A human reviews the evidence before any capital moves.
6. The founder receives a small grant for one critical experiment.
7. Verified progress can unlock a larger, separately documented SAFE investment.

Use this underwriting philosophy: fund the cheapest ethical experiment capable of changing the next investment decision. Optimize for learning per dollar, not hours worked. Negative results are useful when the experiment was executed honestly.

Foundry 01 is a four-founder, 30-day pilot. Each founder may receive a $500 acceptance grant, another $500 for completing the agreed experiment, and approved AI or cloud credits. The pilot takes no equity. The primary metric is verified new paying customers per $1,000 of Rethinksoft cost.

Rethink Score uses ten dimensions totaling 100 points: Founder execution 15, Problem evidence 12, Market 8, Product 8, Technical execution 8, Distribution 15, Traction 15, Capital efficiency 7, Differentiation 5, Risk and integrity 7. Severe fraud, legal, security, sanctions, IP, or integrity concerns override the number.

The visual system should feel like a serious capital and technical institution, not a generic accelerator or AI template. Use deep graphite, warm off-white, restrained signal green, Manrope typography, JetBrains Mono for evidence labels, precise grids, strong editorial type, subtle data-console motifs, and purposeful motion. Avoid purple gradients, fake startup logos, stock photos, and decorative dashboard clutter.

Stack: Astro server output with the Node adapter and better-sqlite3. Use server-rendered HTML and vanilla JavaScript, with no client framework. Retain the SQLite waitlist with honeypot and dedupe. Keep canonical URLs, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, structured data, and build-time Open Graph images using satori and resvg.

Do not build the full application, founder dashboard, payment rails, automated investments, or evidence integrations before the pilot validates the process. Use the site to recruit the first cohort and explain the model honestly. Rethinksoft may continue enterprise AI services privately to finance the experiment, but Foundry is the primary public identity.

MIT license. Public repository. No third-party tracking. No hourly founder compensation. No repayment except fraud or misuse. Founder IP remains with the founder.
